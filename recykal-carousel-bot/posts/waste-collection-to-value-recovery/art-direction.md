# Art direction: From waste collection to value recovery (photographic version)

## Core messages and how they connect
1. **Value is at risk after consumption.** Littering, improper disposal and collection gaps stop packaging reaching recovery.
2. **DRS creates a structured return route.** A refundable deposit encourages empties to be returned to designated collection channels.
3. **Quality of collection and handling shapes recovery.** Contamination and poor handling reduce material quality and recoverable value.
4. **Keep value in circulation.** DRS strengthens return and collection; sorting, processing and recycling turn it into a usable resource.

Progression of the images: **leaking -> returned -> handled well or badly -> processed into a resource.**

## One shared look (applies to every prompt)
Documentary editorial photography; soft natural daylight (overcast or open shade, no harsh sun, no night); 35mm look at eye level; shallow depth of field; natural, slightly muted colour grade with true greens; Indian urban, retail and industrial settings; people shown from behind, from the side or as hands only (no staged posing, no direct-to-camera smiles); plain unbranded PET bottles and aluminium cans; nothing printed that can be read.

## Shot list
| # | Layout | Subject and composition | What it must not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `card`, 3:4 | Kerbside by a city street at early morning: a handful of empty PET bottles and cans resting at the edge of a pavement and storm drain, street receding, passers-by blurred. Items in the lower third, left corner calm. | No heaps, fires or landfill drama: a realistic, modest scene of littering. |
| 2 | `tall`, 3:4 | A customer handing over a few empty bottles to an attendant at a plain collection counter in a retail setting; neatly stacked crates of collected empties behind. Hands in the foreground, faces not the focus. | No branded kiosk or machine, no signage, no suggestion that this is the only way DRS works. |
| 3 | `tall`, 3:4 | Gloved hands at a sorting table at a collection centre: one crate of clean, empty, dry bottles on the left, another of bottles with visible residue and mixed with food-soiled waste on the right. | No exaggerated filth; the difference must be believable and obvious at a glance. |
| 4 | `card`, 3:4 | Close-up of clean shredded PET flakes held in a gloved hand, a processing facility softly out of focus behind. | Must read as the **processing** stage, clearly separate from collection; no implication that collected packaging is automatically recycled. |

Layout variety: card / tall / tall / card, all contained rounded portraits beside the copy (no full-bleed strips).

## Cost and review
About 30 credits per image at 2K (nano-banana-2-1); up to 2-3 attempts per slide if a result is generic, artificial or misleading.

## Outcome (generated and reviewed)
All four shots were generated once with OpenArt (nano-banana-2-1, 2K) and accepted after review: no warped objects, no readable text or brands, modest and believable scenes, one shared look. A light unified grade was applied with `engine/photo_prepare.py` (saturation 0.92 / 0.84 and cooler / 0.78 / 0.90) because slide 2 was warm and slide 3's crates strongly saturated.
Layouts used: 1 `card` (with callout), 2 `tall`, 3 `tall`, 4 `card` (with callout).
Credits used: about 120 (4 images, no re-takes).
