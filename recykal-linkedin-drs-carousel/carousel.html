KNOWING IS NOT THE SAME AS DOING.

The gap between intention and action that a Deposit Refund Scheme can help address.

www.recykal.com

Knowing that something should be recycled does not necessarily mean it will be.

After consumption, what happens to packaging waste is shaped by the consumer’s choice.

It can be retained, returned, discarded, or simply forgotten.

That small moment can determine whether material moves back into the recovery system or leaves it altogether.

www.recykal.com

This is where a Deposit Refund Scheme introduces a different way of thinking about behaviour.

Instead of relying only on awareness or asking people to make the right choice, it builds a system around that choice.

The return becomes a defined action, participation becomes repeatable, and the behaviour has a tangible outcome.

www.recykal.com

The challenge is not always convincing people that recycling matters.

Sometimes, it is creating the conditions that help them act on what they already know.
